What the data says so far

Concentration

Dumping is not everywhere. It clusters.

North city

The heaviest hotspots run through north St. Louis

Baden, Walnut Park, Wells Goodfellow, the Greater Ville and Penrose dominate, along with Dutchtown and Gravois Park in the south. North Broadway along the riverfront shows up repeatedly, matching what reporters found there.

Repeat sites

A few hundred addresses keep coming back

462 addresses have five or more reports since 2022. One block of Maffitt Ave has 42 witnessed-dumping reports across five years. These are the clearest candidates for cameras and enforcement.

Timing

Summer peaks, weekday reporting

Reports climb from March and peak in July and August. Witnessed-dumping reports cluster Monday to Thursday during business hours, which mostly reflects when people call 311.

Data caveat

Report counts are not dump counts
